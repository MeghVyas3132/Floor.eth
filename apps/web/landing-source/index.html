Security ratings for onchain collateral, with bounded enforcement.

Floor continuously checks the effective security of collateral: who can create or release it, which independent verifiers must agree, and how quickly its controls can change. Lenders can use the evidence to reduce new exposure automatically.

01 / Why Floor exists

The collateral can look healthy while its weakest control stays exposed.

A safe-looking token can still depend on one fragile route.

Bridge routes, verifier thresholds, owners, delegates, defaults and upgrade paths all decide who can create or release collateral. The pieces are public, but they are spread across contracts and chains. Floor brings the effective setup into one lender-readable view.

The blind spot

Alerts wait for movement.

A weak setting can stay untouched. Change monitoring has nothing to report when the risk was already there.

The answer

Grade the state that is live.

Floor resolves what is active at a pinned block, then checks it against the lender’s own rule pack.

04 / What makes Floor different

A grade with its state, block and evidence attached.

Floor rates the setup that is actually in force.

Raw settings can inherit defaults, collapse optional thresholds, or point to libraries and controllers that change the effective security. Floor resolves those relationships at one block, then publishes the reads so another party can recompute the grade.
